# ZED PC pose: case-edge truth, v1 against zed_v5 (2026-11-14)

Task: get the true PC pose of the six E1 mapping captures of 2026-11-13 from the ZED image, compare the two ZED PC nets on them, and settle which net is right at the real spot.
Worker on mason, GPU 3, no rig access. No rig config changed. No net trained. Every number has its n. Sim numbers are sim only.

## Verdict

- **Neither net is right at the real spot.** The truth lies between them. Two independent methods agree to 1 mm: the case-edge fit on the ZED frame, and the right wrist camera (port-vision PnP on the panel) through the arm FK.
- At the 2026-11-13 spot (`map_real`), port 2 is at **(228.8, 403.3) mm, +/-1.5 mm** (arm on the same day: 401.5-402.7, plus the 0.7 mm method offset). v1 reads 395.3 (**-8.1 mm**). zed_v5 reads 408.6 (**+5.3 mm**).
- At the 2026-11-03..11 spot (truth y 396.7-399.6), v1 is -2.9..-4.2 mm (day medians) and zed_v5 is +3.1..+5.0 mm.
- **v1 under-reads y beyond about 390 mm.** It saturates near 395-396 mm: the PC is outside its training box (y 345-375 mm). The sim shows the same: v1 dy median -5.6 mm at y 394-404 and -14.3 mm at 404-414 (n = 267, 254 renders). On the six poses v1 follows y moves with a gain of about 0.3; the 9.5 mm move to `map_y_plus` read +1.2 mm. **The v1 gate passed that frame with a 16.3 mm error** (Mahalanobis 18.2 < 21.3).
- **zed_v5 follows moves** (pose-change error <= 1.2 mm, n = 5) but carries a constant offset: y +3.8, x -1.4 mm, yaw +1.7 deg (medians, n = 107 real frames). It is unbiased in the sim (n = 2048). The sim PC geometry is wrong (see below) but explains only +0.3 mm of the y offset. So the offset is a real-appearance gap; the cause is not found.
- **What this means for the runs so far:** every approach since 2026-11-03 started 3-8 mm short in +y (port across). The wrist servo and the align stage absorbed it. The first wrist-net reading rose day by day with the v1 error (below). The earlier argument "v1 within 2 mm" (ZED_V5_20261114.md) assumed a wrist-net gain of 1; the real gain is about 0.6.
- **Next step (proposal, needs the coordinator):** use the case-edge fit as the PC pose (the net gives the start and the presence/OOD gate). It takes 0.1 s on the CPU, converges from +/-20 mm, and agrees with the arm to +0.7 mm (n = 41 runs). It needs the raw HD1080 frame in the capture stage (a chain change, off by default).

## Method

### Case-edge fit (`scripts/perception/fit_pc_edges.py`)

The 2026-11-01 script `fit_pc_edges.py` is not on mason or in git (it was on the rig laptop). This is a new implementation.

- Input: the raw rectified-left HD1080 frame (`frames/top/*_zed_pc.jpg`, or `data/zed_gt/*_zed_left.png`).
- Camera: the deploy calibration, `blind_insertion/configs/real_cameras_duchamp3.json` (top, HD1080 rectified intrinsics, `extrinsics_cam_in_right_base`, refit 2026-10-26).
- Model: the case top face, a rectangle at z 11.5 mm in the right base (sim table -33 mm + 44.5 mm; the left-hand press contact on the real top also reads 11.5 mm).
- Fit: per edge, 40 points over 15-85 % of the length (the corners are rounded). Each point searches along the image normal for the strongest bright-inside step. Then (x, y, yaw) by robust least squares on the point-to-line distance in pixels. Five rounds, search radius 40 -> 10 px.
- When the left gripper hides the left (+y) edge (fewer than 30 of 40 left points within 3 px), the fit uses the panel, right and back edges only ("noL").
- Pose convention: the ZED nets' label (port 2 position in the right base; yaw = insertion direction).

| | Sim (profile v0) | Real (this fit) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top face along the panel | 102.5 mm | **103.5 mm** | ZED edge residuals ~0 px, zed_gt (n = 18 frames) |

The operator's calipers (102.5 x 97 mm, orientation "to confirm") do not match. With the sim geometry, the panel and back edges sit 1.6-3.5 px outside the model on every frame. Geometry `real` gives the physical port 2; geometry `sim` gives the sim label convention (y about 1 mm higher). Tables use `real`.

### Independent check: port 2 from the right wrist camera and the arm (`scripts/perception/zed_arm_port.py`)

For every still `align_measure` before the hand-over: the raw right wrist frames of that measurement, the panel pose by PnP (`deploy/bimanual/port_vision.measure`), port 2 centre in the camera, then the hand-eye pose (`wrist.right.pose_in_link6`) and the believed FK of link_6 at the logged `q_b`. No ZED, no in-hand estimate, no grasp model. Median per run (spread within a run: MAD y 0.03-0.18 mm). Port height comes out at -7.2..-8.6 mm (sim port: -9.5 mm), a sanity check.

Limit: the ZED extrinsics were refit from boards located by the wrist cameras (`fit_zed.py`). So both chains share the right-wrist calibration. That is the frame the arm works in, so it is the truth that matters for the task. The arm yaw is **not** independent: the panel model frame was defined from a v1 ZED yaw on 2026-11-02.

Reading:
- The operator's moves by eye were larger in y than thought: every pose also moved 2-6 mm in y. The x_minus move was 4.8 mm (operator: "closer to 5 mm"). The sideways moves were +9.5 and -12.2 mm. The yaw move was +7.4 deg (absolute 10.3 deg).

## Why zed_v5 is off (sim tests, `scripts/perception/zed_geom_check.py`)

Renders at the capture pose, the v5 box (+/-40 mm, +/-20 deg about (231, 394) mm), default visual DR. n = 2048 per geometry.

| Sim PC | Net | dx, dy, dyaw median | xy err p50 | Gate ok |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sim geometry | zed_v5 | +0.02, -0.02 mm, 0.0 deg | 0.6 mm | 2020 |
| real geometry (`pc_geom_patch.py`) | zed_v5 | **+2.62, +0.33 mm**, +0.12 deg | 2.7 mm | 2018 |
| sim geometry | v1 | -0.47, -2.82 mm; dy -5.6 at y 394-404, -14.3 at 404-414 | 5.9 mm | 1160 |

- The wrong sim case geometry gives zed_v5 a +2.6 mm x error and +0.3 mm y. The real x error has the opposite sign (-1.4 mm). So the geometry is a real gap but not the cause of the +4 mm y offset.
- The left gripper is not the cause either: on zed_gt frames with the left arm folded or parked (no occlusion) zed_v5 reads +3.0..+3.8 mm y, the same as at the capture pose.
- Remaining candidates (not tested): lid and panel appearance (stickers, ports, lid edge highlight), lighting, the black left-gripper cable (not in the sim).
- A constant offset correction of zed_v5 is not enough: leave-one-day-out residual |xy| p50 1.1, p90 3.6, max 5.7 mm (n = 107; the zed_gt x and the 2026-11-13 offset of +5.6 mm against +3.1..+5.0 before).

No quick fix is clear, so I trained no net and wrote no config.

## Proposals

1. **Edge-fit pose estimate (recommended, no training).** In the capture stage: the net (either) gives the start pose and the presence / OOD gate; `fit_pc_edges.fit` on the raw HD1080 frame gives the pose. Accept when: right, panel and back edges each >= 30 of 40 inliers; inlier rms <= 1.2 px (real frames, all edges: 0.16-1.14 px, n = 107; the 1.14 is map_y_plus with the hidden left edge); |fit - net| <= 25 mm (the search range). Use "noL" when the left edge has < 30 inliers. Validated: +0.7 mm y, 0.0 mm x against the arm (n = 41). Needs a chain change (off by default, a config key such as `chain_args.zed_edge_refine`); the coordinator decides. Not done here (chain.py is shared).
2. **Fix the sim PC:** top face 103.5 x 103 mm, port 2 19.8 mm from the centre (31.95 mm from the left edge). `scripts/perception/pc_geom_patch.py` does it for one process (opt-in). A permanent change belongs in the rig profile after the caliper check below.
3. **Net retrain (zed_v6, if a net is still wanted):** the zed_v5 recipe on the corrected PC geometry, plus a fine-tune on real capture frames with edge-fit labels (107 frames, about 25 placements, 6 off-spot), mixed with sim renders; hold out 2026-11-13 for the test. Pass: |error| <= 2 mm y on every held-out frame and pose-change error <= 1 mm. Then the zed_v5 sim sweep and the full-chain dry runs.
4. **Retire v1 for any PC placement with y > 390 mm** (sim and real both show saturation). At the current spot it is 8 mm off, above the 5 mm stop rule.

## Next rig session: what to measure (fewest steps)

1. **Calipers on the PC (2 min, no motion):** top length along the port panel, depth, and port 2 centre from the left edge of the panel. Expected 103.5, 103, about 32 mm. This settles the geometry for the sim and the fit.
2. **Yaw (2 min, no motion):** a square against the PC right side and a tape line on the table along the right-base x axis (two marks from the base plate edge); read the angle (+/-0.5 deg). This settles the 1-2 deg yaw question (edge fit against nets and arm).
3. **One capture-only run at the spot (no approval needed):**
   ```bash
   .venv/bin/python -m deploy.run_bimanual --stage capture --config deploy/config/bimanual_align8_nojump_retry2_rzv2.json --name zedmap_check
   ```
   On mason: `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=3 PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/perception/zed_map.py --dates <date> --out results/placement/zed_map/frames_<date>.json`, then `zed_map_summary.py`.
   - Pass: edge fit inlier rms <= 1.2 px and right edge >= 35 of 40 inliers.
   - Stop (no full runs) if |v1 - edge| > 5 mm in y. At the 2026-11-13 spot it is 8.1 mm. **Move the PC back about 5 mm in -y (toward the right arm) to the 2026-11-10 spot (edge y 398-399 mm)** and capture again; there v1 was -4 mm and K4 seated 10 of 10.
4. Every later run: the arm-port check (`zed_arm_port.py`) needs only the logged align frames; no rig step.
